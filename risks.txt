LEGAL / TRUST
1. Certification claims (BRC, FDA, ISO, FSSAI, APEDA, FIEO, IMC) are shown with NO certificates on hand.
   Buyers, customs or competitors can ask for proof. A false claim can lose a deal or cause legal trouble.
   Owner decision: keep the claims. Action later: collect real certificates + numbers, or remove the ones the
   client does not hold. Site will have a "documents available on request" line and a slot for certificate numbers.
2. Numbers such as "40+ countries" and "30+ years" (founded 1994) - owner confirmed 40+ is true. Keep a record of proof.
3. Brand names (Haldiram's, Parle, MDH etc.) are NOT used. Trademark and "authorised distributor" risk. Only brands
   listed on the old site may be named.
4. Stock photos are generic, not the client's own products. A buyer who visits or receives samples may notice.
   Replace with real warehouse / product / packaging / exhibition photos as soon as possible.
   Any "exhibition highlights" content must be real events, or it must be left as clearly marked placeholders.

SEO / TECHNICAL
5. Nobody can guarantee rank #1 in every country. Plan: strong country pages for the top markets, fast load,
   schema, sitemap, llms.txt. Avoid hundreds of near-duplicate pages (Google treats that as doorway spam).
6. Meta keywords are ignored by Google. Keywords go into real page content, headings and FAQs instead.
7. New domain has no history, so ranking takes months. Submit to Google Search Console and Bing right after launch.
8. The old site (sunitaexports.com) was HACKED (PHP web shells in /admin/upload/). Do not reuse that hosting or
   its files. Ask the client to take it down, or redirect the old domain to the new site (301) once it is clean.
